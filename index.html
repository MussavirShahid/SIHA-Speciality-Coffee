Our Story

Clean ingredients. Honest coffee. Zero compromise.

SIHA started with one belief: specialty coffee should taste incredible and be genuinely good for you. Every cup we pour uses certified organic beans, organic dairy and plant milks, and syrups made without artificial additives — sourced and roasted with the same care whether we're set up at your wedding or a Saturday market in Houston.

100% Organic Sourcing

Beans, milk, and syrups are certified organic — no exceptions.

Fully Mobile Setup

Our cart runs on battery power, so we can pour anywhere in Houston — indoors or out.

Local & Community First

You'll find us at Houston-area markets every week, not just private events.
